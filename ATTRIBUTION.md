**The changes:**
- **What was taken:** bone and cartilage meshes, plus the teeth, eyeballs and humeri as fitting landmarks, picked by name from Z-Anatomy's Blender template.
- **How they were changed:** modifiers applied, triangulated, and converted to a right/up/forward frame in metres.
